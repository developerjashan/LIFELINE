Project Overview

Built for the most difficult vertical rescue environments

Borewell incidents demand speed, visibility, communication, and life-support intervention in spaces too narrow and unstable for direct human access.

What is a borewell?

A borewell is a deep, narrow shaft drilled into the ground to access water. When left uncovered or poorly secured, it becomes a confined vertical hazard that is extremely difficult to access in an emergency.

Why accidents occur

Open borewells, weak safety barriers, low visibility, limited oxygen flow, and the inability to deploy standard rescue equipment quickly make these incidents uniquely dangerous.

Problem statement

Traditional rescue methods struggle with narrow diameters, unstable walls, poor communication, and delayed environmental assessment. A compact robotic intervention platform can stabilize the response window and support the victim immediately.

150-300 mm Typical shaft access constraints
Real-time Sensor, camera, and operator feedback
Multi-support Oxygen, water, communication, stabilization
Zero-entry Human responders stay above the hazard zone

Mission

Preserve life during the most critical early minutes of a confined-space emergency by delivering visibility, situational awareness, and survival assistance directly to the victim.

Objective

Enable rescue teams to monitor live conditions, establish communication, deploy essential supplies, and make safer intervention decisions through one integrated robotic platform.

How It Works

Operational timeline from deployment to monitoring

The rescue workflow is designed to prioritize stabilization, communication, and sustained support in the earliest response window.

  1. 01

    Robot lowered into borewell

    The holding hook and cable guide the system safely into the shaft.

  2. 02

    Camera identifies victim

    Live feed provides first visual confirmation of victim location.

  3. 03

    Sensors collect environmental data

    Temperature, humidity, and motion data inform the rescue team.

  4. 04

    Gripping mechanism stabilizes robot

    Servo arms brace against the shaft wall for operational steadiness.

  5. 05

    Communication established

    Operators coordinate response while reassuring the trapped victim.

  6. 06

    Oxygen supplied

    Emergency oxygen improves survivability in restricted airflow zones.

  7. 07

    Water supplied

    The delivery pipe supports hydration during prolonged rescues.

  8. 08

    Rescue team monitors conditions

    Continuous dashboard data supports safer extraction planning.
